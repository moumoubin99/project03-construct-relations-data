# §2 Ordered diagnostic protocol

For every candidate pair (C1, C2), answer the questions in order.
Stop at the first YES.

## Q-α — Is one of (C1, C2) a generalization of the other?

Diagnostic test: Could a researcher in the relevant literature legitimately
write the sentence "[Specific construct] is [general construct] applied to
[domain]"? If yes, the pair is parent-child.

→ If YES, label is L3 hierarchical. Stop.

Examples that should answer YES here:
- "Purchase intention is behavioral intention applied to purchase behavior."
  → L3.
- "Computer self-efficacy is self-efficacy applied to computer use." → L3 vs
  *general* self-efficacy.
- "Facilitating conditions is the external-resource component of perceived
  behavioral control." → L3.

## Q-β — Are C1 and C2 two sibling instances of a common parent?

Diagnostic test: Is there a higher-abstraction construct C* such that BOTH
C1 and C2 are domain-specific applications of C*, in *different* research
traditions, with no super-sub relation between C1 and C2 themselves?

→ If YES, label is L5 jangle. Stop.

Examples:
- "Continuance intention" (IS post-adoption) and "Repurchase intention"
  (consumer post-purchase) are both children of "post-acceptance/purchase
  intention". → L5.
- "Computer self-efficacy" (IS) and "ICT self-efficacy" (communication) are
  both children of "technology self-efficacy". → L5.

## Q-γ — Do C1 and C2 share the same label?

→ If YES + different referents: L4 jingle. Stop.
→ If YES + same referent: L1 equivalent. Stop.

## Q-δ — Do C1 and C2 share theory roots but differ in focal object?

If neither parent-child nor sibling, but partial overlap exists (e.g.,
different focal entities, partial nomological-position overlap, shared
theoretical genealogy):

→ Label is L2 overlapping. Stop.

Examples:
- "Brand attachment" (marketing) ↔ "Organizational identification" (OB) —
  different focal objects but parallel structure. → L2.

## Q-ε — Is evidence insufficient at confidence ≥ 3 (on 1–5)?

→ Label is L6 insufficient-evidence. Stop. Record what evidence is missing.
